Risk Infrastructure
Circuit Breaker System
Automated price monitoring, trade halts, and withdrawal controls across Crypto, Gold, and FX
Problem Statement
As a multi-asset market maker offering Crypto, Gold (XAU), and FX trading through a proprietary app platform, the firm operates in markets with vastly different volatility profiles — from 24/7 crypto with thin liquidity windows, to macro-sensitive FX pairs and safe-haven gold demand spikes. Today, there is no automated mechanism to detect abnormal price movements or user behaviour and respond in time to prevent cascading losses. A single volatile event can expose the platform to unhedged market-making risk, trigger irrational user behaviour, and result in regulatory and reputational damage. Manual intervention is too slow and operationally unsustainable at scale.
Objectives
Real-time price surveillance
Monitor live feeds across all asset classes and detect abnormal price velocity, spread widening, or feed staleness within seconds.
Asset-level circuit breakers
Automatically halt or restrict trading on a specific instrument when market conditions breach defined thresholds — independently per asset class.
User-level circuit breakers
Apply per-account controls — trade pauses, withdrawal halts, or reduce-only mode — when a user's activity or loss profile triggers risk limits.
Platform-wide safety net
A global breaker triggered when aggregate platform exposure or drawdown exceeds firm-wide risk thresholds, escalating to senior ops and risk teams.
Scope of Coverage
Crypto
BTC, ETH, SOL and major pairs. Highest volatility. 24/7 trading with no natural market close. Requires tightest latency SLAs.
Gold (XAU/USD)
Spot and synthetic gold. Prone to sharp safe-haven demand spikes during macro events. Breakers tied to intraday % moves.
FX Pairs
Major and minor pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY). Tight spreads with event-driven dislocation risk around central bank announcements.
Enforcement Actions
Trade Halt Withdrawal Freeze Spread Widening Reduce-Only Mode Quote Suspension Compliance Flag
Proposed Approach
Build a dedicated Circuit Breaker Service that sits between price feed ingestion and the order/withdrawal management layer. It applies a configurable, tiered rules engine — evaluating conditions at the asset level and user level independently — and enforces halts with sub-100ms latency. All events are logged to an immutable audit trail for compliance and post-incident review.
Delivery Phases
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4
Foundation
Price feed pipeline, anomaly detection, asset-level breakers (L1/L2), ops monitoring dashboard.
Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8
User Controls
Per-user risk tiers, loss and withdrawal breach triggers, alert and notification layer, compliance flagging.
Phase 3 · Weeks 9–12
Platform Hardening
Platform-wide aggregate breaker, config UI for ops, audit logs, full UAT in staging environment.
